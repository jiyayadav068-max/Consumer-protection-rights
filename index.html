Detailed Indian Consumer Acts & Legislation
Primary Legislation

Consumer Protection Act (COPRA), 2019

The core legal framework replacing the 1986 Act:

  • CCPA Regulator: Central authority to recall hazardous goods, order class-action refunds, and penalize false advertising.
  • Product Liability: Manufacturers and sellers are legally liable for injuries caused by defective products.
  • E-Commerce Mandates: Requires online platforms to display country of origin and acknowledge complaints within 48 hours.
  • Mediation: Establishes alternative dispute resolution cells attached to consumer courts.
Food Safety Law

Food Safety & Standards Act (FSSAI), 2006

Governs all food processing and distribution:

  • 14-Digit License: Every packaged food must feature an authentic FSSAI registration number.
  • Adulteration Penalties: Imposes heavy fines and imprisonment for mixing harmful chemical additives in food.
  • Veg/Non-Veg Dots: Mandatory green circle or brown/red square indicators on all food packages.
Quality Standards

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Act, 2016

Ensures product quality and standard certification:

  • ISI Mark: Compulsory certification mark for safety-critical goods like gas cylinders, electrical appliances, and helmets.
  • Gold Hallmarking: Unique 6-digit HUID code mandatory for gold jewelry to prevent purity fraud.
  • AGMARK: Quality seal for agricultural products (spices, honey, vegetable oils).
Weights & Measures

Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Regulates weights, measures, and retail packaging declarations:

  • MRP Enforcement: Charging above the Maximum Retail Price printed on packages is illegal.
  • Mandatory Declarations: Net quantity, manufacturing date, expiry date, and manufacturer customer care must be clearly printed.
3-Tier Pecuniary Jurisdiction under COPRA 2019

District Commission

Jurisdiction: Handles claims where the value of goods and services paid does NOT exceed ₹1 Crore.

State Commission

Jurisdiction: Handles claims exceeding ₹1 Crore up to ₹10 Crore.

National Commission (NDRCD)

Jurisdiction: Handles major claims exceeding ₹10 Crore.
